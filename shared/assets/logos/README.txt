========================================================================
OFFICIAL LOGOS DIRECTORY
========================================================================

Place your logo image files (PNG, SVG, or JPG) in this folder.
For example:
  - doh.png
  - province-davao-del-norte.png
  - bagong-pilipinas.png

Then open `shared/js/logos-config.js` and list the filenames:

window.OFFICIAL_LOGOS = [
  '../shared/assets/logos/doh.png',
  '../shared/assets/logos/province-davao-del-norte.png'
];

They will automatically display cleanly formatted at the top of every slide!
========================================================================
